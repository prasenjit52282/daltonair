Indoor air can be up to 5× more toxic than outdoor air and you can't see it.

Invisible spikes in CO₂ and toxic particulates build up in homes and offices every day, unnoticed. PoWear is a wearable safety device that senses hazardous air in real time and shows users exactly where to act, before it affects their family's health, with zero cameras and zero microphones.

Step 01 · Sense

Detection Engine

Trained on 89.1M readings from 30 real homes over 13,646 hours, PoWear knows exactly what a dangerous CO₂ or particulate spike looks like before it becomes harmful.

Step 02 · See It

PoWear Wearable

A wristband that turns invisible pollution into a glowing AR bubble users can point a fan at, clearing a danger zone in seconds, not hours. 3.8× faster hotspot clearance in testing.

Step 03 · Stay Safe

On-Device AI

Learns a household's risky habits: frying, poor ventilation, overnight buildup from air chemistry alone, and warns before they recur. No cloud, no privacy risk.
