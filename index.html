The Detection Engine Behind PoWear

DALTON: How PoWear Knows Danger When It Sees It.

Most air monitors guess using a single sensor in one corner of a room. PoWear doesn't guess. Its detection model is trained on DALTON, 89.1 million readings from multi-sensor grids across 30 real homes over 13,646 continuous hours, so it recognizes exactly how everyday habits turn safe air dangerous.

Real Dangers We Found In Real Homes

The Deep-Frying Migration Hazard

Indian culinary habits frequently involve long-term deep frying with spices. This generates massive spikes in sub-micron particulate matter (PM₂.₅) and Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs). Unlike boiling, which stays contained in the kitchen, frying aerosols aggressively migrate into bedrooms within 15 minutes.

The Overnight Ghost Effect

Unwashed dishes or food scraps left overnight in kitchen sinks cause a continuous, 8-hour rise in ambient VOC and Ethanol (C₂H₅OH) concentrations. Even with no active occupants, indoor air toxicity steadily degrades until the sink is sanitized.

The Architectural Trap

Floor plans with isolated kitchen structures trap VOCs aggressively. While CO₂ levels might quickly normalize via basic window ventilation, dangerous VOCs linger significantly longer, getting caught in architectural blind spots.

The Wearable Safety Device

Meet PoWear v1.

Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) is odorless and silently pools in "dead zones" around desks and beds. You open a window and assume the room is fine, but the hazard is still sitting right next to you. PoWear is the wristband that catches it, shows you exactly where it is, and tells you how to clear it.

A Warning You Can Actually Act On

A generic "air quality bad" notification doesn't tell you what to do. That's why PoWear shows the hazard exactly where it's forming. The moment PoWear detects CO₂ exceeding 1200 PPM on your wrist, it pushes the danger zone straight to your phone or AR headset — so you know precisely where to point a fan.

  • Volumetric Color Coding: Blue spheres indicate baseline air (<800 PPM), green represents moderate accumulation (800–1200 PPM), and pulsing red spheres warn of hazardous stagnation (>1400 PPM).
  • Targeted Airflow Nudging: Guided by visual spheres, users do not just open windows blindly; they position desk fans to blow directly into the stagnant red bubble cluster, reducing clearance times from hours to seconds.

The Smart AI Inside PoWear

PoHAR: Protection Without Surveillance.

A safety device that watches you through a camera isn't safety!! it's a new risk. PoHAR is the on-device AI that learns your household's risky patterns from air chemistry alone. Every time someone cooks, cleans, or leaves dishes overnight, PoWear recognizes the pattern and warns you, without ever recording you.

The Decentralized Edge Intelligence Pipeline

Because standard IoT microcontrollers have tiny memory footprints (4MB Flash), they cannot process raw high-frequency sensor streams centrally. PoHAR introduces three novel algorithmic primitives running natively on ESP32 hardware:

01. Time-Frequency Consistency (TF-C) Embeddings

On-device self-supervised learning models compress multivariate time-series air data (PM₂.₅, CO₂, VOC, Temp, RH) into compact, low-dimensional spatial vectors without cloud offloading.

02. Ultra-Lightweight RAFT Consensus

Distributed sensor nodes continuously elect a local "Cluster Leader" using heartbeat sync timeouts. Only the leader executes the final activity classification, reducing household energy draw by 68%.

03. Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types (Set-CvRDT)

Ensures seamless data merging across nodes even if household Wi-Fi drops or packets are delayed, maintaining 97.41% general daily activity accuracy.
